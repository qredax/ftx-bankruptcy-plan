# 7.14 — Requirements and notices

{% hint style="info" %}
Page 68 of the plan. Section heading: Tax Identification, KYC and OFAC Certifications.
{% endhint %}

## What can be required

A holder entitled to receive distributions must, on request, deliver an IRS form W-9 or W-8, an OFAC certification, and identity verification.

For individuals that means name, date of birth, address, identity documents, nationality, phone, email, occupation, bank or wallet details, taxpayer number, and facial liveness verification.

For institutional holders it can include company name, registration details, taxpayer number, principal address, the nature of the business, entity size, source of wealth and source of funds, annual revenue and profit, authorised signer, ultimate beneficial owners, and directors or members of management. For each beneficial owner and director, the same individual verification collected from a natural person.

## The clocks

Thirty days from the request. If the requirements are not satisfied, a second request is issued. Sixty days from that second request.

Past that, the holder may forfeit the right to receive distributions. The published wording is "may", not "will", which leaves the decision with the Trust.

Neither period runs from a calendar date. Both run from a request.

## When a request counts as received

This is the provision that decides everything above.

> For all purposes related to this Section 7.14, the Plan Administrator and the Distribution Agent are entitled to conclusively rely on the delivery of the notice to the last known address and last known email address on file with the Claims Agent without obligation to verify such address and any such requests delivered to such address shall be deemed to be received.

Deemed received. Not received.

A request sent to an address the holder no longer has access to is delivered as a matter of the plan, and the period on it runs. "I never got anything" is answered in the document before anyone reads it.

## What follows practically

The address and email on file with the claims agent are not administrative details. They are where the clocks start.

For anyone acquiring a claim, this argues for a written obligation on the seller to keep access to that mailbox until settlement, to forward anything that arrives, and not to change the address on file without consent.

## What it means for a corporate holder

The institutional list is worth reading before choosing a structure rather than after. A trust may request beneficial owners, directors, source of capital and annual revenue, with individual verification for each named person, on a thirty plus sixty day timetable.

Assembling that after the request arrives is late.
