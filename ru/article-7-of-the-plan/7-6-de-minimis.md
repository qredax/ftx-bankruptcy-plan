# 7.6 — The ten dollar floor

{% hint style="info" %}
Pages 64–65 of the plan.
{% endhint %}

> the Plan Administrator and the Distribution Agent shall not be required to make Distributions in Cash or payments of less than $10. Any Holder of an Allowed Claim on account of which the amount of Cash or other property to be distributed is less than $10 shall be forever barred from asserting such Claim against the Debtors, the Estates, the Plan Administrator, the Wind Down Entities or any of their property.

Not deferred. Barred.

## Why it is not only about small claims

Large claims pay in instalments. The final instalment can land below the line, and that portion is treated the same way as a small claim: not paid, and barred.

So the floor applies at the level of each distribution, not at the level of the claim.

## The second floor

There is also a threshold on access rather than on payment. FTX support documentation lists the conditions for reaching step 8 of the portal, and one of them is that the claim be above the ten dollar de minimis amount.

A claim below the floor does not open the step at all.

## Connected to reversion

Section 7.8 treats a distribution not made under 7.6 as unclaimed property that reverts to the estate. The two provisions work together: the payment is not made, and the amount goes back.
