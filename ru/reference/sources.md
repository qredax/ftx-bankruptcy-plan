# Sources

## The plan

Second Amended Joint Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization of FTX Trading Ltd. and Its Debtor Affiliates, D.I. 26404-1, case 22-11068, United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware.

Filed 8 October 2024. Effective 3 January 2025.

All quoted wording on these pages is from that document. Page numbers refer to the pagination printed on the document itself.

## The docket

The case docket and filings are public at the claims agent's site.

Objections to claims are separate filings with their own numbers, and each states its ground. That ground predicts the outcome more reliably than anything shown on a portal status line.

## Support documentation

FTX publishes support articles covering distribution service providers, the conditions for reaching step 8, the distributions dashboard, and guidelines on sales of claims. These are updated without notice, so anything taken from them should carry the date it was read.

## What is not sourced here

Secondary market prices. Cumulative figures beyond the ones on these pages. Any count of restricted jurisdictions, because the published lists differ between the US and Bahamas processes and between dates.

Where a figure moves, it is either dated on the page or left out.
