## What decides the size of a round

Not a calendar. Each round is funded when the court approves a reduction in the reserve held against disputed claims, releasing cash for allowed ones.

The third round followed a court-approved reduction of the disputed claims reserve from $6.5B to $4.3B. The fourth followed a further reduction of about $2.2B.

Which is why the intervals have run from three to six months and the amounts have moved by a factor of ten.

## Why no end date is published

There is a record date for the next round and nothing beyond it. The size and timing of each payment depend on how many disputed claims get resolved, and that is not something anyone schedules in advance.

For context on how far estimates have drifted: in September 2023 the case's lawyers, billing around $50M a month, were reported as expecting the process wrapped up by the following July.
