# Overview

This is a working breakdown of Article 7 of the Second Amended Joint Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization of FTX Trading Ltd. and its Debtor Affiliates, filed as D.I. 26404-1 in case 22-11068 in the District of Delaware and effective 3 January 2025.

Article 7 governs how distributions are made: who qualifies for a round, what happens to a payment nobody collects, what the Trust owes a holder in return, and the conditions that have to be met before anything is released.

Most coverage of the case summarises the outcome. This is the wording itself, quoted directly, with the page number in the document so it can be checked.

## What is here

Five sections, one page each.

**7.4** sets the record date window and governs delivery, including what happens when a claim is transferred close to a record date, and when the administrator may withhold a payment.

**7.6** is the floor below which nothing is paid at all, and the claim for that amount is barred.

**7.8** covers unclaimed distributions, the six-month reversion, and the notice obligation the Trust carries in the other direction.

**7.10** covers setoffs, which run in one direction only.

**7.14** sets out the pre-distribution requirements and the notice rule that decides when a request counts as received.
